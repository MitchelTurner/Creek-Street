Design review

That board is the Borough's.

CreekStreet.org is about the street and the projects coming to it. The Creek Street Architectural Design Review Board is a separate body. Its members, its purpose, and its meeting notices are published by the Ketchikan Gateway Borough.

Official page

Start at the Borough.

The Borough's board page is the place for who sits on the board, when it meets, and what it was set up to do. Meetings are open to the public and held in the Assembly Chambers at the White Cliff Building, 1900 First Avenue.

Questions about a building in the historic district, a proposal, or a meeting go to the Planning Department: planning@kgbak.us, 907.228.6610.
