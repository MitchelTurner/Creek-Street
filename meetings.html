The record

The Borough keeps the official record.

Minutes are taken by the Borough's Platting and Zoning Clerk. They are drafted after a meeting and adopted by the board at the following one, so the first written account of any meeting is provisional by design — it can change when the board takes it up.

Summaries on this site are written from those draft minutes and labeled draft until adoption. They are a plain-language account, not the record itself. For official notices, agendas, and the authoritative record, go to the Borough.
