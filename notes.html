The record

The Borough keeps the official record.

Minutes of the design review board are taken by the Borough's Platting and Zoning Clerk. They are drafted after a meeting and adopted at the following one, so the first written account of any meeting can still change.

Notes on this site are a plain-language reading of public material, written so the projects on the street have a source. They are not the record, and they are not a publication of the board. For notices, agendas, members, and adopted minutes, go to the Borough.

Meetings of that board are open to the public and held in the Assembly Chambers at the White Cliff Building, 1900 First Avenue. This site does not set the dates and does not send the notices. The Planning Department does: planning@kgbak.us, 907.228.6610.

What shows up

The useful part is often off the agenda.

A formal agenda is a poor way to find out what the street needs. Bears on the creek, trash, restrooms, the entrance, where summer crowds bunch up — neighbors raise those whether or not they are a design-review item. When they bear on the street, they go on the project list, marked for what they are.
