Projects

What's being built, repaired or proposed on Creek Street, where each item stands, and when it last changed.

  • In discussion

    Public restrooms

    The condition and availability of the public restrooms that serve Creek Street's visitors. The Borough's Public Works department is the next point of contact on their status.

    Updated Aug 17
    Raised Aug 17
  • Active

    Design review for exterior changes

    Changes to building exteriors in the Creek Street district go through public design review again, after about a decade without meetings. Owners planning work on facades, signs, railings or decks should expect a review step.

    Updated Aug 17
    Raised Aug 17
  • In discussion

    Reaching owners and organizations

    An effort to let property owners, businesses and Ketchikan organizations know how the review process works, so projects on the street are planned with it in mind from the start.

    Updated Oct 10
    Raised Aug 17
