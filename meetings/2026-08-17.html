Note · public meeting

August 17, 2026 — the first seated meeting in years.

Assembly Chambers Called to order 4:57 pm Adjourned 5:53 pm

Who was there

All five members came.

Present: Bolshakoff, Hayley Chambers, Stephen Reeve, Turner, and Tim Whiteley. The Borough publishes the current roster, with contact details, on its own board page.

Officers

The board elected its officers.

All three were elected without objection.

  • Stephen Reeve Chair
  • Tim Whiteley Vice Chair
  • Hayley Chambers Secretary

Officer roles are from the August 17, 2026 draft minutes — not yet adopted.

What members raised

Nothing was decided. Plenty was named.

A first meeting is for finding out what is on people's minds. These are things members brought up, not positions the board has taken. The same items are on the project list, which is where status lives.

Safety came up first.

Members identified trees in danger of falling as a hazard to Creek Street buildings. The visitor entrance to Creek Street was described as dangerous where pedestrians and vehicle traffic mix. Members said the historic district needs a neighborhood plan, and that congestion and pinch points have to be considered before adding amenities like restrooms.

Then the wider street.

Members expressed interest in collaborating with local entities such as the Visitors Bureau. Concerns included pedestrian congestion during the tourism season and flooding during high tides and heavy rain. Members discussed dispersing visitors more widely around the community, and observed that the boardwalk's current design may already be regulating pedestrian flow — widening parts of it could push more people through, not fewer. Some storeowners are asking visitors to wait outside because of maximum capacity.

Public comment

Transit came to say the same thing about a plan.

Kyan Reeve, Ketchikan Gateway Borough Transit director, described running into conflicts with a plan to move a bus stop from in front of the Museum to somewhere closer to the Creek Street entrance. He encouraged the board to work toward an area plan, and said the Transit Department will look to work with the Planning Department and Planning Commission on an updated area plan for the area.
