Priorities

What's actually in front of the board.

These came out of the first meeting. The list will grow and change. Some of it isn't the board's to fix — that's marked.

How to read this

Four labels, meaning what they say.

A label is not a promise. Most of this list is at the stage of having been said out loud in a public meeting.

  • On an agenda

    Scheduled for discussion at a specific meeting.

  • Raised

    Members brought it up; nothing scheduled yet.

  • Not ours to fix

    Belongs to another department or body; the board can carry it there.

  • Open question

    The board doesn't yet know whether this is within its purview.

The list

Everything on the plate.

  • Public restrooms

    On an agenda

    Identified at the first meeting as the main near-term priority. The September meeting is set to take it up after the board reaches out to City Public Works. Members also flagged that where restrooms go matters as much as whether they go in — Creek Street already has pinch points.

    From the August 17, 2026 meeting — draft minutes, not yet adopted by the board.

  • Trees in danger of falling

    Raised

    Members identified hazard trees as a safety risk to Creek Street buildings.

    From the August 17, 2026 meeting — draft minutes, not yet adopted by the board.

  • The entrance to Creek Street

    Raised

    Members described the visitor entrance and the approach to it as dangerous where pedestrians and vehicle traffic combine.

    From the August 17, 2026 meeting — draft minutes, not yet adopted by the board.

  • Congestion on the boardwalk

    Raised

    Pedestrian congestion during the tourism season, with storeowners asking visitors to wait outside at capacity. Members noted the boardwalk's current width may be doing useful work regulating flow, and that widening sections of it could increase congestion rather than relieve it.

    From the August 17, 2026 meeting — draft minutes, not yet adopted by the board.

  • A neighborhood plan for the Historic District

    Raised

    Members said the district needs one. The Transit director made the same point in public comment and said his department will work with the Planning Department and Planning Commission toward an updated area plan. Multiple bodies now converging on the same need.

    From the August 17, 2026 meeting — draft minutes, not yet adopted by the board.

  • Flooding at high tide and in heavy rain

    Raised

    Named among the district's standing problems when members went through what the street contends with.

    From the August 17, 2026 meeting — draft minutes, not yet adopted by the board.

  • Spreading visitors around the community

    Not ours to fix

    Members discussed dispersal as part of the larger picture. Wider than design review, and wider than this board — but it shapes every other item on this list.

    From the August 17, 2026 meeting — draft minutes, not yet adopted by the board.

  • Bears on the creek

    Open question

    Members asked whether bear control could fall within the board's purview. That question hasn't been answered. In the meantime it's one of the things people bring to the workshop.

    From the August 17, 2026 meeting — draft minutes, not yet adopted by the board.

Add to it

Something missing from this list?
